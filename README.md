# psia-irea-hackathon

A place to store artefacts and outputs from the November 2026 Intelligent Retrofit Energy Advisor Hackathon in the Whitechapel Building.

## Team IREA: public estate retrofit investigation

Team IREA's static evidence prototype helps a public budget holder identify government estate records worth investigating for funded energy efficiency work. It traces 53,009 Cabinet Office building rows to 2,267 provisional investigation prompts. Public ownership is a working assumption, and the Tower Hamlets pilot adds local site and deprivation checks that have not been applied nationally.

The 2,267 records are leads for checking evidence, not approved retrofit projects. Ownership, measured energy, feasible measures, costs, savings and authority to carry out works still need confirmation.

**Sites access:** The earlier [national Sites preview](https://government-estate-retrofit-2026.crpage.chatgpt.site/) and [Tower Hamlets Sites preview](https://tower-hamlets-retrofit-notebook.crpage.chatgpt.site/) remain owner-only. The GitHub Pages deployment above is the public version.
